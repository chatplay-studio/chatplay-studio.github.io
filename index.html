How it works

Our game software reads the public live chat of our own YouTube streams through the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API), detects game commands in the messages, and shows the result on stream, with the viewer's public display name next to their action. We do not ask viewers to sign in, we do not access any private YouTube data, and we do not sell or share any data.

By playing (typing in the chat of our live streams) you also use YouTube, so the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy apply. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms.
