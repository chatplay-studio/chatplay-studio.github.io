Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information ChatPlay Studio ("we") processes when you watch or play our interactive live games on YouTube (the channels Chat Lava, Chat Alchemy, Chat Gravity and Chat Tower), and when you visit this website. Contact: studio.chatplay@gmail.com.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

Our game software uses the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3) to read the public live chat of our own YouTube live streams, to find which of our streams is live, and to read the public subscriber count of our own channels. By interacting with our live streams you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Information we access

Only public information that you choose to post in the live chat of our streams:

  • your public YouTube display name (channel name);
  • the text of your public chat messages;
  • for Super Chats, Super Stickers and memberships: the event type and the amount as displayed publicly by YouTube.

We do not ask you to sign in with Google, we do not use OAuth to access your account, and we do not access any private data (email address, watch history, subscriptions, etc.).

3. How we use it

  • to turn chat commands into game actions in real time;
  • to show your display name on stream next to your action (for example "@name poured lava");
  • to show game leaderboards (for example "top builders") on our streams.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose, and we never sell, rent or share it with third parties.

4. Storage and retention

  • Chat messages are processed in memory and are not stored.
  • For leaderboards, we store your display name and your game scores (numbers of actions) on our game server. A display name that has not been seen in our chat for 30 days is automatically deleted together with its scores.
  • The public subscriber count of our own channels is kept in memory only, to celebrate new subscribers on stream.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can stop the processing at any time by not posting in our live chats. To have your display name and scores deleted immediately, email studio.chatplay@gmail.com with your display name: we will delete them within 7 days. Because we never access your Google account, there is no access to revoke; if you wish, you can still review apps connected to your Google account on the Google security settings page.

6. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers, and does not collect personal data. It is hosted by a static hosting provider that may keep standard technical server logs.

7. Children

Our streams follow YouTube's age rules. We do not knowingly collect any information beyond the public chat information described above.

8. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, with a new "last updated" date.
